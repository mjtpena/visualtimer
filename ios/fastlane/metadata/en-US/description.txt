VisualTimer brings the classic time-awareness of an analog clock face to your iPhone and iPad. Instead of staring at a digital countdown, you see a vivid colored arc shrinking in real time — making the passage of time immediately visible and intuitive. Whether you set a 25-minute Pomodoro sprint or a 5-minute break, the sweeping arc and precise clock hand keep you anchored to the moment without demanding your full attention.

Key features include one-tap preset buttons (1, 5, 10, 25 minutes) for instant setup, a customizable analog clock face (three sizes, flip/mirror mode), dark and light themes, smart audio alerts (loud tick every 5 minutes, gentle tick for the final 10-second countdown, and a distinct timer-end chime), and satisfying haptic feedback when your session ends. All your preferences — theme, size, sound — are saved automatically so the app is ready exactly how you left it.

VisualTimer is ideal for focused work sessions, classroom activities, kitchen timers, workout intervals, meeting time-boxes, and mindfulness practices. It runs reliably in the background with local notifications so you never miss the end of a session, even if you switch apps. Clean, distraction-free design means there is nothing between you and your time.
