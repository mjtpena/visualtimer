Version 1.1.0 brings significant reliability and usability improvements:

• Quick-preset buttons (1, 5, 10, 25 min) — start a timer in one tap
• Background timer drift fixed — the clock stays accurate even when you switch apps
• Local notifications — get alerted when your timer ends, even in the background
• Haptic feedback on timer completion
• Smarter audio — ticks only in the last 10 seconds and at 5-minute marks (no more constant ticking)
• Arc now scales correctly to any duration, not just 60-minute sessions
• App preferences (theme, size, sound, flip) now persist across launches
• VoiceOver accessibility labels added throughout
• Stability improvements and crash fixes
