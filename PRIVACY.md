# Privacy Policy for VisualTimer: Countdown Clock

**Last updated:** April 2026

## Overview

VisualTimer: Countdown Clock ("the App") is developed by Michael John T. Peña.

## Data Collection

**The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- No user accounts or registration is required.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.
- No data is sent to any server.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive permissions are requested.

## Local Storage

The App may store user preferences (such as timer settings) locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

## Audio

The App plays audio feedback using your device's built-in audio capabilities. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
