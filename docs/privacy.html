Privacy Policy

App: VisualTimer: Countdown Clock
Developer: Michael John T. Peña
Last updated: April 2026

Overview

VisualTimer is a simple countdown timer. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

No data of any kind is collected. Specifically:

  • No user accounts or registration is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No data is sent to any server, ever.
  • No permissions for location, contacts, camera, or microphone are requested.

Local Storage

The app may save your timer preferences locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is never accessible to the developer or any third party.

Audio

The app plays audio cues using your device's built-in audio system. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected from anyone, no special treatment of children's data is required. The app is safe for all ages.

Changes

If this policy is updated, the revision will be posted at this URL.
